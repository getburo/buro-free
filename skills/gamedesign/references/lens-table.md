# Schell's Full Table of Lenses — reach-for reference

SKILL.md keeps 20 working lenses — the ones needed on almost every project. Schell has **over a hundred**: each a narrow question for a situation the working lenses don't name. Open this file when the panel hits a problem (an auction, character status, bots, a loot box, an investor pitch) and none of those asks the right question.

**This is not a checklist.** Don't run down the table top to bottom looking for what's wrong — that contradicts Schell's own method: he warns that lenses are applied one at a time, to a specific problem, not as a deck run all at once. Find the lens whose subject is closest to what's broken, ask its question of the design, move on.

⚠️ **Go by the lens's name, not its number.** Numbers drift between editions (the 1st and 3rd editions diverge; the full official numbering here is the 3rd edition, 2019, "Table of Lenses"). Numbers in this table are for orientation within the table only — cite lenses to the panel by name: "the Lens of Cooperation," not "lens #44."

A lens is a **question**, not a rule. Three lenses are already unpacked in depth, with criteria and quotes — see `canon.md`: **the Toy**, **Meaningful Choices** (dominant strategy lives there too), **Triangularity**. Their questions are included here too for completeness, but go there for the breakdown.

## Experience and intent

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Emotion | What emotions would I like my player to experience? Why? |
| Essential Experience | What experience do I want the player to have — and what is essential to it? |
| the Venue | What type of venue best suits the game I'm trying to create? |
| Surprise | What will surprise players when they play my game — and do the rules give them ways to surprise each other? |
| Fun | What parts of my game are fun? Why — and which parts need to be more fun? |
| Curiosity | What questions does my game put into the player's mind, and what am I doing to make them care about these questions? |
| Endogenous Value | What is valuable to the players in my game — and how does that relate to their motivations? |
| Problem Solving | What problems does my game ask the player to solve — and does it generate new ones so players keep coming back? |
| the Elemental Tetrad | Is my game design using elements of all four types — and are they in harmony, reinforcing each other? |
| Holographic Design | What elements of the game make the experience enjoyable, and which ones detract from it right now? |
| Unification | What is my theme — and am I using every means possible to reinforce it? |
| Resonance | When I describe my game to people, what ideas get them really excited? |
| Infinite Inspiration | What is an experience I have had in my life that I would want to share with others — and how did I put it into my game? |
| the Problem Statement | What problem, or problems, am I really trying to solve — and is a game really the best solution? |
| the Eight Filters | Does this game feel right, is it novel enough, will it sell, and will playtesters enjoy it enough — all eight at once? |
| Risk Mitigation | What could keep this game from being great — and how can I stop that from happening? |
| **the Toy** *(deeper — canon.md)* | If my game had no goal, would it be fun at all? |
| Passion | Am I filled with blinding passion about how great this game will be — and if I've lost it, can I find it again? |

## The player

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| the Player | In general, what do they like, what don't they like, and what would I want to see in a game in their place? |
| Pleasure | What pleasures does your game give to players? Can these be improved? |
| Flow | Does my game have clear goals, and do challenges keep pace with the player's growing skill? |
| Needs | On which levels of Maslow's hierarchy is my game operating — competence, autonomy, relatedness? |
| Motivation | What motivations do players have to play my game — internal or external, toward pleasure or away from pain? |
| Novelty | What is novel about my game — and is there novelty throughout, not just at the beginning? |
| Judgment | What does your game judge about the players, how does it communicate that — and do players feel the judgment is fair? |

## Space and time

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Functional Space | Is the space of this game discrete or continuous, and how many dimensions does it have? |
| the State Machine | What are the objects in my game, what are their attributes and possible states — and what triggers each state change? |
| Time | What is it that determines the length of my gameplay activities — and does the game end too early or drag on too long? |
| Secrets | What is known by the game only, by all players, or by only some — and would changing that improve my game? |
| Emergence | How many verbs do my players have, how many objects can each act on, and how many ways can players achieve their goals? |
| Action | What are the basic actions in my game, what are the strategic actions — and am I happy with the ratio of the two? |

## Rules, risk, and choice

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Goals | Is the ultimate goal clear to players — and do they ever get to choose their own goals? |
| Rules | How do the foundational rules differ from the operational ones — and who enforces them? |
| Skill | What skills does my game require from the player — and does it make the experience I want? |
| Expected Value | What is the actual chance of an event occurring versus the perceived chance — and am I happy with the resulting expected value of each choice? |
| Chance | What in my game is truly random, and what parts just feel random? |
| Fairness | Should my game be symmetrical or asymmetrical — and why? |
| Challenge | Are my challenges too easy, too hard, or just right — and do they grow as the player succeeds? |
| **Meaningful Choices** *(deeper — canon.md)* | Are the choices I'm asking the player to make meaningful — and are there any dominant strategies in my game? |
| **Triangularity** *(deeper — canon.md)* | Do I have triangularity now — a choice between the safe-and-small and the risky-and-large? |
| Skill vs. Chance | Are my players here to be judged (skill) or to take risks (chance) — and is my game serious or casual as a result? |
| Head and Hands | Are my players looking for mindless action or an intellectual challenge — and can I give them a choice of the two? |
| Competition | Does my game give a fair measurement of player skill — and can novices meaningfully compete with experts? |
| Cooperation | Do my players have enough opportunity to communicate — and how could that communication be enhanced? |
| Competition vs. Cooperation | If "1" is competition and "10" is cooperation, what number should my game get? |
| Reward | What rewards is my game giving out now — and are players excited by them, or bored? |
| Punishment | Why am I punishing the players, what do I hope to achieve by it — and could a reward achieve the same effect? |

## Balance, difficulty, economy

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Simplicity/Complexity | What elements of innate complexity do I have — and could any be turned into emergent complexity instead? |
| Elegance | How many purposes does each element of my game serve — and can the ones with only one or two be combined or cut? |
| Character | Is there anything strange or funny in my game that players talk about excitedly? |
| Imagination | What details do I provide that inspire imagination — and which ones stifle it? |
| Economy | How can my players earn money, what can they buy — and is money too easy or too hard to get? |
| Balance | Does my game feel right? Why or why not? |
| Accessibility | How will players know how to begin — do I need to explain it, or is it self-evident? |
| Visible Progress | Is there enough progress visible in my game, or is some of it hidden where it could motivate instead? |
| Parallelism | Is there a bottleneck where a stuck player can't proceed — and can I add a parallel challenge for them to work on instead? |
| the Pyramid | Is there a way all the pieces of my puzzle can feed into a single challenge at the end? |
| the Puzzle | What are the puzzles in my game — and should there be more of them, or fewer? |

## Interface and response

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Control | When players use the interface, does it do what is expected? If not, why not? |
| Physical Interface | What does the player pick up and touch — and can this be made more pleasing? |
| Virtual Interface | Does the interface let players do what they want — and will it become invisible with practice? |
| Transparency | Does the interface work well in all situations, including stressful ones? |
| Juiciness | Is my interface giving the player continuous feedback for their actions — and does the player's action create second-order motion? |
| Primality | What parts of my game are so primal an animal could play? Why? |
| Channels and Dimensions | What data need to travel to and from the player — and what channels are available for that? |
| Modes | What modes do I need in my game, why — and can any be collapsed or combined? |
| Metaphor | Is my interface already a metaphor for something else — and am I making the most of it? |
| Feedback | What do players need to know at this moment — and what do they want to know? |

## Pacing and structure of experience

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Moments | What are the key moments in my game — and how can I make each one as powerful as possible? |
| the Interest Curve | If I draw an interest curve of my experience, how is it generally shaped — does it have a hook and a grand finale? |
| Inherent Interest | What aspects of my game will capture the interest of a player immediately, without any buildup? |
| Beauty | What elements make up my game — and how can each one be more beautiful? |
| Projection | Is there anything in my game players can relate to, and a place they've always wanted to visit? |
| the Story Machine | When players have different ways to achieve a goal, new stories can arise — how can I add more of these choices? |
| Simplicity and Transcendence | How is my world simpler than the real world — and what transcendent power do I give the player in exchange for the challenge I removed? |
| the Hero's Journey | Does my story have elements of a heroic story — and would it feel hackneyed if it matched that structure too closely? |
| the Weirdest Thing | What's the weirdest thing in my story — and how do I make sure it doesn't confuse or alienate the player? |
| Story | Does my game really need a story — and why will players be interested in this one? |
| the Obstacle | What is the relationship between the main character and the goal — why does the character care about it at all? |
| Freedom | When do my players have freedom of action — and do they feel free at those times? |
| Help | Within the context of the game, who exactly is the player helping? |
| Indirect Control | Ideally, what would I like the players to do — and what nudges them there without breaking their sense of freedom? |
| Collusion | What do I want the player to experience — and how can the characters help deliver that without betraying their own goals in the game world? |

## World and characters

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Fantasy | What fantasy does my world fulfill — and who does the player dream of being inside it? |
| the World | How is my world better than the real one — and can more than one gateway lead into it? |
| the Avatar | Is my avatar an ideal form the player can easily project themselves into? |
| Character Function | What roles do my characters need to fill — and can one character fill more than one? |
| Character Traits | What traits define my character, and how do they show up in words, actions, and appearance? |
| the Interpersonal Circumplex | Are there gaps in the chart of character relationships — and would it be better if they were filled? |
| the Character Web | How, specifically, does each character feel about each of the others — and are there unaccounted-for connections? |
| Status | What are the relative status levels of the characters — and how does the game let the player express their own status? |
| Character Transformation | How does each character change over the course of the game — and is that change communicated clearly enough? |
| Inner Contradiction | Is there anything in my game that contradicts its own purpose? |
| The Nameless Quality | Does my design have a special feeling of life, or do parts of it feel dead — and what would bring them to life? |
| Presence | Is my player experiencing a sense of presence — and what in the game strengthens or breaks it? |
| Atmosphere | Without using words, how would I describe the atmosphere of my game? |

## Audience, society, ethics

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Spectation | Is my game interesting to watch? Why or why not? |
| Cheatability | Can players cheat at my game — and would anyone notice? |
| Friendship | What kind of friendships are my players looking for — and at what moment do they become friends? |
| Expression | How am I letting players express themselves — and what ways am I missing? |
| Community | What conflict is at the heart of my community — and why do players need each other at all? |
| Griefing | What systems in my game are easy to grief — and how can I make grief boring rather than banned? |
| Love | Do I love my project? If not, how can I change that? |
| the Team | Is this the right team for this project — and is it ultimately able to unify around decisions? |
| Documentation | What do we need to remember while making this game — and what needs to be communicated to everyone else? |
| Playtesting | Why exactly are we doing this playtest — and how will we get the information we need? |
| Technology | Am I using this technology foundationally or decoratively — and if decoratively, should I use it at all? |

## Business and the point of the project

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| the Crystal Ball | What will this be like in two years? In four? In ten — and why? |
| Utopia | Does my game advance the state of the art — and does it make the world a little better? |
| the Client | What does the client say they want — and what do they really want, deep down? |
| the Pitch | What do the people I'm pitching to need to know about my game for the decision to be theirs? |
| Profit | Where does the money go in my game's business model — and how much will this game actually make, and why do I think that? |
| Transformation | How can my game change players for the better — and how can it change them for the worse? |
| Responsibility | Does my game help people? How? |
| the Raven | Is making this game worth my time? |
| Your Secret Purpose | Why am I really doing this? |
